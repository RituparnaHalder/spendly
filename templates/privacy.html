Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we also store the expense records you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide and maintain the service: signing you in, showing your spending summaries and category breakdowns, and responding to your support requests. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and kept only for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and the associated records at any time, and we will remove them within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical information such as your IP address when the font files are requested. We do not share your expense records with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team.
